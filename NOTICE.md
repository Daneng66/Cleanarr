# Attribution

Cleanarr's rule model, safety design (dry-run default, approvals with live
revalidation, retention rules, rejection memory, run lease, append-only audit
trail) and rule vocabulary are derived from the Library Cleanup feature of
[arr-dashboard](https://github.com/Kha-kis/arr-dashboard) by its contributors,
used under the MIT License:

    MIT License
    Copyright (c) 2025 arr-dashboard contributors

Cleanarr is a from-scratch reimplementation of that feature as a standalone,
single-purpose app; it does not copy arr-dashboard's source files.
